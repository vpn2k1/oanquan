# O Quan Viet — English listing

## Google Play

**App name:** O Quan Viet

**Short description:** Play Vietnamese O an quan with 2–4 players, solo or with friends

**Full description:**

O Quan Viet brings the traditional Vietnamese game Ô ăn quan to phones and tablets. Choose a board for your group, sow pebbles, capture mandarins, and plan each move to win.

• Play with 2, 3, or 4 players on rectangular, triangular, or square boards.
• Face the computer at Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty, or take turns with friends on one device.
• Learn the rules step by step, then try a practice game against the computer.
• Use hints and undo, and save a game to continue later.
• Track achievements and game statistics stored on your device.
• Choose a playing surface, light or dark theme, sound, and display accessibility options.

The two-player board follows traditional Ô ăn quan rules. The three- and four-player boards extend the same sowing and capturing mechanics. Each mandarin is worth ten seeds; the highest score wins when the game ends.

The game supports Vietnamese and English. Current play modes are against the computer or with friends taking turns on one device.
